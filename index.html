Quantum AI for Medical Imaging — 8-Qubit QCNN on DermaMNIST

Supervised by Dr. Vipin Venugopal (Amrita School of AI) & Dr. Murugadass K. · Presented at NCRTST-2026 (IIT Madras & PSG Tech)

Designed and implemented a hybrid 8-qubit Quantum Convolutional Neural Network (QCNN) using PennyLane and PyTorch for binary skin lesion classification (Actinic Keratoses [AKIEC] vs. Basal Cell Carcinoma [BCC]) on the DermaMNIST dataset.

01
Dimensionality Compression Pipeline (784 → 16)

Engineered a classical autoencoder reducing 28×28 input images (784 features) into a compact 16-dimensional latent vector suitable for amplitude/angle encoding on an 8-qubit register.

02
8,247× Parameter Reduction with Superior Accuracy

Achieved ~60% test accuracy with only 51 trainable quantum parameters, outperforming the classical CNN baseline (~55% accuracy using 420,610 parameters).

03
National Conference Poster Presentation
